# Studio website research

Reviewed 19 November 2026 through official public pages. Structural research, not a claim of exhaustive visual or mobile audits. No competitor text, logos, screenshots, project data or price points have been copied into Aldwyn’s website.

| Reference | Observed structure | Decision for Aldwyn |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [MadeByShape](https://madebyshape.co.uk/) | Clear agency introduction, work near the top, distinct services and about pages, recurring start-project action | Plain-language website offer in the hero; dedicated services/portfolio/studio routes; consistent project-planning CTA |
| [KOTA](https://kota.co.uk/) | Separate services and work, case studies describing decisions, service-to-contact path | Give a project room for brief, reasoning and scope. Use Aldwyn’s restrained geometric identity and current service boundaries |
| [UWRH Digital portfolio](https://uwrhdigital.com/our-work/) | Separates client projects from concepts with visible project labels | Show actual context and status on every Aldwyn entry, including our self-initiated website in progress |

Aldwyn’s distinguishing direction: dark editorial typography, the selected extended AL monogram, angular supporting geometry and a quieter portfolio that explains the work. No award, team-size, client-result or revenue claims. One honest project is sufficient initially; filters would add friction with one entry.
